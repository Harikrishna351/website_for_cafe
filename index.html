Our Story

Founded in 2024

Crafted with Intention

Velvet Roast emerged from a simple belief: that coffee deserves reverence. We are not merely a café; we are curators of moments, architects of atmosphere, and passionate advocates for the perfect cup.

Every bean is selected through direct relationships with farmers who share our commitment to excellence. We roast in small batches, allowing the unique character of each origin to shine through.

Our space is designed as a sanctuary from the ordinary—a place where time slows, conversations deepen, and the aroma of possibility fills the air.

Quality

Only the top 1% of beans make it to our roaster. We accept nothing less than perfection.

Sustainability

From farm to cup, we ensure ethical practices that honor both people and planet.

Community

We believe coffee connects people. Our space is designed for meaningful encounters.
